
### Collaborative threat modeling

#### Readability
- revisit threat model docs after x months (12?) to check if they still make sense, can they be interpreted by someone not present? Are threats still relevant or do they need to be updated?
#### Avoiding silos
- "architecture archaeology"
- Offer personal value - What's in it for me?

#### Managing misaligned interests
- Get mgmt buy-in -> make business case
- provide shortcuts for devs 
- WIIFM -> threat modeling lets you avoid work

#### Avoiding passive participation
Invite challenge
- having an ugly incomplete model will invite feedback. You need other's perspective!

### The science of metrics

Goal-question-metric framework
- start with goals
- then formulate the questions to measure progress
- metrics that help answer those questions

Impact - CVSS
Likelihood - EPSS + KEV
EPSS changes over time

Questions: what are properties of a successful appsec program. What are the quality attributes of the SDLC?
e.g. Reduce the time needed to fix high risk vulnerabilities in production

**Metric properties to consider**
- precision: granularity and margin of error
- consistency of measurement: does it stay constant over time

**Metric validity**
- content validity
